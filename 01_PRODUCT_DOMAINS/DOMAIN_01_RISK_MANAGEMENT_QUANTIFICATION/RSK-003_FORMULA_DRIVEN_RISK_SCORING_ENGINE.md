## Executive Summary

The **Formula-Driven Risk Scoring Engine** is the quantitative decision engine that transforms enterprise risk attributes into consistent, governed enterprise risk scores.

Using configurable weighting across **Likelihood**, **Impact**, and **Velocity**, the engine automatically calculates inherent and residual risk scores whenever relevant risk information changes. This eliminates manual calculations, improves scoring consistency across the organization, and provides the quantitative foundation for downstream capabilities including:

- Enterprise Risk Register  
- 5×5 Risk Heatmap  
- Executive Dashboards  
- Board Reporting  
- AI Governance  
- Predictive risk analytics  

Within Vindexion, the Risk Scoring Engine is not simply a calculation utility. It is a **governed enterprise service** responsible for:

- Maintaining scoring consistency  
- Supporting tenant-specific methodologies  
- Preserving historical score versions  
- Enabling future AI-assisted optimization  
- Maintaining human oversight and decision integrity

# Commercial Narrative

## Customer Problem

Enterprise organizations frequently rely on inconsistent, spreadsheet-based scoring methodologies that produce subjective results, manual recalculations, and limited transparency into how overall risk scores are derived.

As risk portfolios grow, maintaining scoring consistency across business units becomes increasingly difficult, reducing confidence in executive reporting and enterprise decision-making.

---

## Customer Outcome

The **Formula-Driven Risk Scoring Engine** provides a governed, deterministic scoring model that automatically calculates inherent and residual risk scores whenever likelihood, impact, or velocity values change.

This delivers consistent enterprise-wide scoring, eliminates manual calculations, improves governance transparency, and provides the quantitative foundation for downstream capabilities including the Risk Heatmap, Executive Reporting, AI Governance, and future quantitative risk analytics. This complements the canonical capability of configurable weighting, automatic recalculation, and versioned score history.

---

# Enterprise Intelligence  
## Enterprise World Model (VEWM™) Contribution

The **Formula-Driven Risk Scoring Engine** provides the quantitative decision layer for governed Risk Objects within the **Vindexion Enterprise World Model (VEWM™)**.

Rather than maintaining independent scoring records, the engine continuously evaluates governed enterprise risk data using configurable weighting across **likelihood**, **impact**, and **velocity** to produce the inherent and residual scores that drive downstream product experiences.

Every calculated score remains directly connected to its originating enterprise objects, preserving **traceability**, **governance**, **explainability**, and **historical scoring integrity**. This aligns with the canonical definition of deterministic scoring, automatic recalculation, tenant-specific weighting, and versioned score history.
