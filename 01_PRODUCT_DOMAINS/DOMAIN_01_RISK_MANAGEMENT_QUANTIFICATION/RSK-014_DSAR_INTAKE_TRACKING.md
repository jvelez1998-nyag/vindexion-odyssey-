## Executive Summary

The **DSAR Intake & Tracking** capability provides a centralized workspace for managing Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs) throughout their complete lifecycle, from intake and identity verification through fulfillment, regulatory reporting, and case closure.

Built upon the Data Inventory & Mapping capability (RSK-012) and the Privacy Risk Register (RSK-013), the feature connects requests to enterprise data assets, business processes, applications, regulatory obligations, third parties, and supporting evidence, creating a complete chain of custody for every request.

Rather than relying on email, spreadsheets, or disconnected ticketing systems, Vindexion enables organizations to manage privacy rights requests through standardized workflows with full visibility into request status, ownership, deadlines, approvals, and fulfillment activities.

The capability establishes the foundation for AI-assisted request classification, intelligent data discovery, automated workflow routing, predictive SLA monitoring, and continuous privacy operations.

## Commercial Narrative

### Customer Problem

Organizations often struggle to manage Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs) across email, spreadsheets, ticketing systems, and manual processes.

As privacy regulations expand, organizations must verify identities, locate personal information across multiple systems, coordinate fulfillment across business units and third parties, and respond within strict regulatory deadlines. These fragmented processes increase operational costs, regulatory risk, and customer dissatisfaction.

### Customer Outcome

The **DSAR Intake & Tracking** capability provides a centralized workspace for receiving, validating, routing, fulfilling, and monitoring Data Subject Access Requests.

Organizations gain standardized workflows, real-time SLA monitoring, complete audit trails, stronger regulatory compliance, improved operational efficiency, and greater transparency throughout the entire request lifecycle.

### Competitive Differentiation

Many GRC and privacy platforms manage DSARs as isolated case-management workflows.

Vindexion differentiates this capability by treating every DSAR as a governed Enterprise Object within the Enterprise World Model (VEWM™), connecting requests directly to enterprise data assets, applications, AI systems, third parties, business processes, regulatory obligations, evidence, and executive reporting.

## Enterprise Intelligence

### Enterprise World Model (VEWM™) Contribution

The **DSAR Intake & Tracking** capability serves as the enterprise privacy operations layer within the Vindexion Enterprise World Model (VEWM™).

Rather than treating Data Subject Access Requests as isolated cases, Vindexion models each request as a governed Enterprise Object connected to individuals, enterprise data assets, processing activities, applications, AI systems, third-party processors, regulatory obligations, fulfillment activities, evidence, and executive reporting.

This architecture creates complete end-to-end traceability across the privacy request lifecycle while strengthening governance, operational efficiency, and regulatory defensibility.

### Connected Enterprise Objects

- Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs)
- Enterprise Data Assets
- Data Subjects
- Processing Activities
- Applications & Systems
- AI Systems & Models
- Third-Party Vendors
- Business Units
- Regulatory Obligations
- Privacy Risk Register
- Evidence Repository
- Fulfillment Activities
- Executive Reporting

These relationships enable every privacy request to be fully traceable from initial intake through fulfillment, regulatory reporting, and long-term evidence retention.

### AI Opportunities

- **AI Capability Portfolio**
- **Request Classification:** Automatically classify privacy requests and determine applicable regulatory requirements.
- **Data Discovery:** Identify systems, applications, and repositories containing relevant personal information.
- **Workflow Routing:** Recommend responsible teams based on request scope and enterprise ownership.
- **SLA Monitoring:** Predict requests at risk of exceeding regulatory deadlines.
- **Fulfillment Recommendations:** Suggest response packages and supporting evidence.
- **Executive Privacy Summaries:** Generate concise operational and compliance summaries for leadership.

### Decision Authority

AI recommendations remain advisory.

Identity verification, request approval, fulfillment decisions, regulatory interpretations, evidence validation, and final response delivery require authorized human review and governance approval.
