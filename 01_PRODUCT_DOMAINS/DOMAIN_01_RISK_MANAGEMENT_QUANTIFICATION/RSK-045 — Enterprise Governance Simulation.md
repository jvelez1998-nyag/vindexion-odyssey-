# Executive Summary

The **Enterprise Governance Simulation** capability enables leaders to test governance strategies, risk decisions, control changes, regulatory responses, resource reallocations, and enterprise operating scenarios before those changes are implemented in the real organization.

Traditional GRC systems are primarily descriptive.

They show:

- Current risks
- Current controls
- Current issues
- Current remediation status
- Current compliance posture

Vindexion extends that model into a forward-looking simulation environment.

Powered by the **Enterprise World Model (VEWM™)** and Digital Twin services, RSK-045 creates a governed virtual representation of enterprise governance conditions that can be used to model:

- Risk changes
- Control changes
- Regulatory events
- Resource constraints
- Business growth
- Cyber incidents
- M&A activity
- AI adoption
- Third-party disruption
- Policy changes
- Governance investment changes
- Autonomous governance expansion

The capability allows executives to compare alternative futures before committing capital, resources, authority, or organizational change.

The result is a governed enterprise simulation environment that enables leadership to ask:

> **What happens if we do this?**

before the enterprise actually does it.

# Digital Twin Fidelity

Every simulation should communicate the fidelity of the underlying model.

## High Fidelity

Strong enterprise data, validated relationships, mature historical evidence, and well-supported quantitative assumptions.

## Moderate Fidelity

Good enterprise coverage with some modeled or estimated relationships.

## Emerging Fidelity

Material gaps remain in enterprise relationships, historical evidence, or assumptions.

## Experimental

Scenario is primarily exploratory and should not be relied upon for consequential decisions without additional analysis.

This prevents visual sophistication from being mistaken for predictive certainty.

Representative variables might include:

- Loss probability
- Loss magnitude
- Control effectiveness
- Recovery time
- Remediation duration
- Resource availability

- Regulatory implementation cost

```text
Expected Loss Reduction

25th Percentile:    $5.9M
75th Percentile:   $11.7M
95th Percentile:   $16.8M

Probability of achieving target:
72%
```

This provides a more credible representation of uncertainty.
