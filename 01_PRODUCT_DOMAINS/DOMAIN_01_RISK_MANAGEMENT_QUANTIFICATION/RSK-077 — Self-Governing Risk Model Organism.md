# Canonical Definition

RSK-077 extends the bounded recalibration capability introduced in RSK-402 beyond adjustments to model weights.

The capability allows Vindexion to identify when the underlying structure of a risk model may no longer adequately represent enterprise reality and to propose or execute approved structural adaptations within tightly governed human-defined boundaries.

Representative structural changes may include:

- Addition of a new modeled risk dimension
- Modification of relationships between risk factors
- Introduction of a new risk category
- Adjustment of model segmentation
- Addition or removal of scoring variables
- Reconfiguration of dependency assumptions
- Modification of aggregation logic
- Adaptation to systemic enterprise or market change

The canonical requirement is explicit:

# **THE MODEL MAY EVOLVE ONLY INSIDE HUMAN-DEFINED LIMITS, AND EVERY STRUCTURAL CHANGE MUST BE EXPLAINABLE, REVERSIBLE, AND SUBJECT TO HUMAN HALT AND ROLLBACK.**

# Executive Summary

Traditional risk models assume that the structure used to understand risk remains sufficiently stable.

The enterprise defines:

```text
RISK CATEGORIES
      +
SCORING FACTORS
      +
WEIGHTS
      +
CORRELATIONS
      +
AGGREGATION RULES
```

and then periodically revises those elements through human-led model governance.

That approach is appropriate when enterprise reality changes relatively slowly.

But increasingly:

- New technologies appear
- AI introduces unfamiliar risk relationships
- Business models change
- Markets restructure
- Regulations redefine obligations
- Geopolitical conditions shift
- Threats evolve
- Dependencies become more complex
- Previously separate risk categories begin interacting

A model may therefore remain mathematically stable while becoming conceptually stale.

RSK-077 addresses that problem.

```text
ENTERPRISE REALITY CHANGES
        ↓
MODEL PERFORMANCE / COVERAGE DEGRADES
        ↓
STRUCTURAL CHANGE DETECTED
        ↓
ADAPTATION PROPOSED
        ↓
GOVERNANCE BOUNDARY CHECK
        ↓
SIMULATION + VALIDATION
        ↓
HUMAN REVIEW / AUTHORIZED CHANGE
        ↓
MONITOR
        ↓
ROLL BACK IF NECESSARY
```

The strategic thesis is:

# **A LIVING ENTERPRISE REQUIRES A RISK MODEL CAPABLE OF LEARNING WHEN ITS OWN STRUCTURE NO LONGER FITS REALITY.**

# Part 3 Closing Perspective

RSK-077 is not a feature that allows a model to rewrite itself.

It is a governed architecture for allowing a model to recognize when its representation of risk may be failing.

That requires more than an adaptive algorithm.

The system must know:

- What the model currently believes
- How the model is structured
- Which parts are protected
- Where performance is deteriorating
- Whether simple recalibration can solve the problem
- Which structural alternatives exist
- How those alternatives perform
- What enterprise decisions would change
- Whether the change remains explainable
- Whether the prior state can be restored
- Who has the authority to approve or halt it

Only then does structural adaptation become enterprise-grade intelligence.

The model may become increasingly capable of questioning its own assumptions.

But the system architecture must ensure one principle never drifts:
