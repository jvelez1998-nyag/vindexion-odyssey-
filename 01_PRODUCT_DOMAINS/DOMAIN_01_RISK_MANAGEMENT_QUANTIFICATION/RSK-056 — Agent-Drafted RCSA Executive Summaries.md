# Canonical Product Reference

The canonical capability enables an agent to produce a narrative executive summary from a completed RCSA campaign for board or committee reporting.

Three requirements are foundational:

- The narrative is generated from actual campaign results.
- Material claims remain traceable to supporting campaign data.
- Human review and sign-off are required before distribution.

RSK-056 expands this foundation into a decision-grade executive reporting experience.

---

# Executive Summary

Enterprise RCSA campaigns can generate hundreds or thousands of individual responses, findings, ratings, exceptions, and remediation actions.

Executives do not need another data dump.

They need to understand:

> **What changed, what matters, why it matters, and where leadership attention is required.**

RSK-056 converts completed RCSA campaign data into concise, evidence-grounded executive narratives.

The agent performs the analytical assembly.

The human retains editorial judgment and publication authority.

---

# Strategic Purpose

Traditional RCSA reporting often creates a costly final-mile problem.

Risk teams spend significant time:

- Consolidating business-unit results
- Identifying material themes
- Translating technical findings
- Drafting committee narratives
- Reconciling narrative claims with source data

RSK-056 compresses that work into an AI-assisted reporting workflow while preserving evidence, traceability, and human accountability.
