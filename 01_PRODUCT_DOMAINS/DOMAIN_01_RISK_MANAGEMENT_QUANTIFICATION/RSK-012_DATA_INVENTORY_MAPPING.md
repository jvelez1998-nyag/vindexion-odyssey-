## Executive Summary

The **Data Inventory & Mapping** capability provides organizations with a centralized, governed inventory of enterprise data assets, enabling visibility into what data is collected, where it resides, how it flows, who owns it, and which regulatory obligations apply.

Rather than maintaining disconnected spreadsheets or manual inventories, Vindexion creates a living enterprise data catalog that connects business processes, applications, databases, third parties, information assets, and regulatory requirements through the Enterprise World Model (VEWM™).

The capability supports privacy, security, governance, and operational risk programs by providing a trusted foundation for Data Privacy Impact Assessments (DPIAs), DSAR fulfillment, regulatory reporting, AI governance, third-party risk management, and enterprise data lineage.

As the platform evolves, Data Inventory & Mapping becomes the enterprise knowledge foundation for intelligent data governance, automated discovery, regulatory mapping, AI-assisted classification, and continuous compliance monitoring.

## Commercial Narrative

### Customer Problem

Organizations often lack a complete understanding of what data they collect, where it resides, how it moves across the enterprise, and which regulatory obligations govern it.

Data inventories are frequently maintained in disconnected spreadsheets, become outdated quickly, and fail to provide the visibility required for privacy, security, AI governance, regulatory compliance, and executive decision-making.

## Enterprise Intelligence

### Enterprise World Model (VEWM™) Contribution

The **Data Inventory & Mapping** capability serves as the enterprise information layer within the Vindexion Enterprise World Model (VEWM™).

Rather than treating data inventories as disconnected spreadsheets, Vindexion models every data asset as a governed Enterprise Object linked to business units, applications, databases, AI systems, third parties, enterprise risks, controls, regulatory obligations, and information owners.

This architecture establishes a continuously evolving enterprise knowledge graph that supports privacy, security, AI governance, operational resilience, and executive decision-making.

### Connected Enterprise Objects

- Enterprise Data Assets
- Business Units
- Legal Entities
- Applications & Systems
- Databases
- Data Owners & Stewards
- Enterprise Risk Register
- Control Library
- Policies & Standards
- Regulatory Obligations
- AI Systems & Models
- Third-Party Vendors
- Executive Reporting

These relationships provide complete visibility into enterprise information assets while preserving end-to-end governance and regulatory traceability.

### AI Opportunities

- **AI Capability Portfolio**
- **Data Classification:** Automatically classify structured and unstructured information based on sensitivity and regulatory requirements.
- **Metadata Enrichment:** Recommend business context, ownership, and missing metadata.
- **Data Lineage Discovery:** Identify relationships and movement between enterprise systems.
- **Regulatory Mapping:** Recommend applicable privacy and industry regulations for each data asset.
- **Governance Gap Detection:** Identify missing ownership, controls, or policy coverage.
- **Executive Data Summaries:** Generate concise summaries of enterprise data governance posture.

### Decision Authority

AI recommendations remain advisory.

Classification approvals, ownership assignments, regulatory mappings, governance decisions, and inventory certification require authorized human review before becoming part of the governed enterprise inventory.
