## Executive Summary

The **Data Privacy Impact Assessments (DPIA)** capability provides a governed workspace for evaluating the privacy impacts of new technologies, business processes, products, vendors, AI systems, and data processing activities before implementation.

Built upon the Data Inventory & Mapping (RSK-012), Privacy Risk Register (RSK-013), and DSAR Intake & Tracking (RSK-014) capabilities, the feature guides organizations through a structured assessment process that identifies privacy risks, evaluates regulatory obligations, recommends safeguards, and documents risk treatment decisions.

Rather than relying on manual questionnaires and disconnected documents, Vindexion centralizes DPIAs into a standardized workflow that links assessments directly to enterprise data assets, business processes, AI systems, controls, third parties, and regulatory requirements.

The capability establishes the foundation for AI-assisted privacy assessments, intelligent regulatory guidance, automated control recommendations, predictive privacy analysis, and continuous privacy governance.

## Enterprise Intelligence

### Enterprise World Model (VEWM™) Contribution

The **Data Privacy Impact Assessments (DPIA)** capability serves as the enterprise privacy decision layer within the Vindexion Enterprise World Model (VEWM™).

Rather than treating DPIAs as standalone documents, Vindexion models each assessment as a governed Enterprise Object connected to enterprise data assets, processing activities, AI systems, business units, third-party vendors, privacy risks, controls, regulatory obligations, mitigation plans, approvals, and executive reporting.

This architecture creates complete traceability between proposed processing activities, governance decisions, implemented safeguards, and ongoing regulatory compliance while strengthening enterprise privacy intelligence.

### Connected Enterprise Objects

- Data Privacy Impact Assessments (DPIAs)
- Enterprise Data Assets
- Processing Activities
- Business Units
- Legal Entities
- Applications & Systems
- AI Systems & Models
- Third-Party Vendors
- Privacy Risk Register
- Control Library
- Policies & Standards
- Regulatory Obligations
- Mitigation Activities
- Executive Reporting

These relationships ensure every privacy assessment remains connected to the enterprise ecosystem throughout its lifecycle.

### AI Opportunities

- **AI Capability Portfolio**
- **Assessment Guidance:** Recommend applicable privacy questions based on the processing activity.
- **Risk Identification:** Detect potential privacy risks using enterprise context and historical assessments.
- **Control Recommendations:** Suggest safeguards aligned with identified risks and regulatory obligations.
- **Regulatory Mapping:** Recommend applicable privacy laws and compliance requirements.
- **Assessment Quality Review:** Identify incomplete responses, missing evidence, or governance gaps.
- **Executive Assessment Summaries:** Generate concise decision-ready summaries for approvers.

### Decision Authority

AI recommendations remain advisory.

Assessment approval, residual risk acceptance, regulatory interpretation, control selection, mitigation approval, and authorization to proceed require authorized human review and governance approval.
