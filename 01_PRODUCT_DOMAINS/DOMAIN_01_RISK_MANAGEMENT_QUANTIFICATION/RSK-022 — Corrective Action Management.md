# Executive Summary

The **Corrective Action Management** capability governs the complete lifecycle of corrective actions from planning through validated completion, ensuring enterprise issues are resolved efficiently, consistently, and with measurable business impact.

Building upon Enterprise Issue Management (RSK-021), this capability transforms corrective actions into governed Enterprise Objects connected to issues, risks, controls, regulations, policies, AI systems, business processes, vendors, evidence, and organizational objectives through the **Vindexion Enterprise World Model (VEWM™).**

Rather than simply assigning remediation tasks, Vindexion continuously orchestrates execution through intelligent workflow automation, dependency management, AI-assisted prioritization, progress monitoring, evidence validation, and executive oversight.

The capability establishes the operational foundation for enterprise remediation, continuous improvement, governance accountability, and organizational resilience.

# Commercial Narrative

## Customer Problem

Many organizations identify issues successfully but struggle to execute corrective actions consistently. Remediation activities are often managed through email, spreadsheets, project plans, and disconnected ticketing systems, making it difficult to coordinate work, track accountability, monitor progress, validate completion, and demonstrate governance effectiveness.

Without disciplined corrective action management, issues remain open longer, remediation costs increase, regulatory findings recur, and organizational risk accumulates.

---

## Customer Outcome

The **Corrective Action Management** capability provides a centralized operational workspace for planning, executing, monitoring, validating, and governing enterprise corrective actions.

Organizations gain complete visibility into remediation execution, stronger ownership, accelerated issue resolution, measurable accountability, improved audit readiness, and greater confidence that corrective actions effectively address underlying risk.
